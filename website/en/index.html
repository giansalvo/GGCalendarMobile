Your Google Calendar, on Android, even offline.

GGCalendar is a calendar for Android that syncs in both directions with one or more Google Calendar accounts. Events always available, reminders that really ring, no ads.

Download See the features

Free · Android 8.0 or later · Italian, English, Spanish and German · Version 1.0.5

What it does

A complete calendar, made for people who live by appointments and do not want to miss any.

All the views

Schedule, day, 6 days, week, month and month with schedule. Drag and resize events right in the grids.

Google sync

Two-way, with several accounts. Changes made offline go out as soon as the network is back. If there is a conflict, Google’s version stays and your copy is saved as “(conflict)”.

Reminders and alarm

Notification, a full-screen alarm that rings for up to 2 minutes, or an email sent by Google. A list of active alarms with the time left.

Recurring events

Full recurrence rules and editing of “this event only”, “this and following events” or “the whole series”, as in Google Calendar.

Guests and Google Meet

Add guests, a Meet link, a colour and a time zone to every event.

Export and share

Export to .ics and CSV, sharing of an event, import of .ics files and local-only calendars.

Birthdays from contacts

Optional: shows your contacts’ birthdays in a separate calendar, only if you grant the permission.

Light and dark theme

Interface in Italian, English, Spanish and German, 24-hour clock, first day of the week and many other settings.

Your data stays yours

In short. Full details are in the privacy policy.

  • No server of the author: the app talks only to Google.
  • No ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • Events and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • Google Calendar data is neither sold nor shared with third parties.
  • You can disconnect an account at any time, from the app or from Google.
  • The code is public and verifiable (GPL v3 or later licence).

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Frequently asked questions

 

Do I need a Google account?

No. Without an account you can use local calendars, which exist only on the phone. The account is needed to sync Google Calendar.

Why does Google show “unverified app” the first time I sign in?

Access to calendar data is waiting to complete Google’s verification. If the warning appears, check that the app is GGCalendar by Giansalvo Gusinu, tap “Advanced” and then “Go to GGCalendar”. Until verification is complete, Google limits the number of users who can authorise the app.

How do I delete my data?

Remove the account from the app menu: its events are deleted from the phone. To revoke the authorisation too, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. The author keeps no data: there is nothing to delete elsewhere.

Do reminders ring even when the phone is in battery saver?

The app uses Android’s exact alarms and guides you to grant the permissions they need. Some manufacturers apply aggressive limits to background apps: if reminders arrive late, exclude GGCalendar from battery optimisation. Do not rely on a single app for critical deadlines.

Is it connected to Google?

No. GGCalendar is an independent app that uses the public Google Calendar APIs. Google and Google Calendar are trademarks of Google LLC.
